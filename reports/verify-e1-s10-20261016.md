(`07-seed-force.log`), 6 photos + 1 dump added — the flag names its effect in the plan line:

Honest note: the first `--force` measurement piped the script into `head -8`. `head` closed the
pipe after the upload lines, the script was killed by SIGPIPE before its dump, and my
`test … && echo` assertion failed silently (the `&&` swallowed it, `set -e` did not fire on the
non-final command). The rerun above, unpiped, is the evidence — the pipe's behaviour is a
property of the pipe, not of the script.

compare after trimming one trailing slash, so `…_Data//`, `…_Data/.`,
