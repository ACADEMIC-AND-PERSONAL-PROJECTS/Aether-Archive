## Not covered — residual risk

- **A real device.** No physical finger and no iOS Safari anywhere in this run; Chromium was driven
  synthetically over CDP. `100dvh` and safe-area insets are unverifiable headless (no dynamic toolbars, no
  notch).
- **The decoder guarantee outside Chromium.** "Decoded before reveal" is measured in headless Chromium
  (the `naturalWidth` 1200 / `blob:` src above); other engines are assumed, not measured.
- **The empty archive's browser path.** It is covered at the HTTP layer (a `404` + `application/problem+json`
  with `"code":"empty-archive"`) and in jsdom through the app's real providers (`data-state="empty"`), but no
  browser capture of that state exists in this slice — and its words are E3-S4's anyway.
- **The backend suite.** Not run: this slice touches no backend file. The backend served as a probe server
  and its responses matched `_docs/openapi.yml` for `Photo`.
- **The dev data directory.** `AETHER_DATA_DIR` in the gitignored root `.env` resolves to a placeholder that
  does not exist, so the archive the capture shows is a throwaway one (one generated 1200×1800 PNG in
  `/tmp/e3s1-hero-data`, database `aether_e3s1_hero`). The splash's `error` state is what a developer sees
  against the real dev configuration — an environment finding, recorded here, not a defect of this slice.
- **Slice B's behaviour** (freshness, the tap target, the gesture) is deliberately absent: no test and no
  doc in this branch claims it, and ADR-021 lists the two deferred decisions with their measurements.
