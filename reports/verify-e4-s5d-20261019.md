## What this report does not measure

- **A real river.** E5-S1 does not exist; the optimistic entry is asserted through the cache in
  `api/dumps.test.tsx` and the jsdom end-to-end, not on a screen. The probe proves the navigation
  after the 201, not what a feed would show.
- **A real archive.** By design: ADR-024 says this story's criterion names a mocked API, the backend
  was frozen, and every body the probe answers with is a measured shape (E2-S2's problem bodies,
  E4-S5C's dump).
- **Visual regression.** The captures are one-shot evidence, not a pixel baseline.
