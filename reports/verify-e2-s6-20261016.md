## 2. The theme algorithm — what "by construction" means

- **Background** = the palette's darkest colour, l clamped to [0.10, 0.18], C ≤ 0.05, hue kept —
  the image's hue survives, pure black never ships. **Surface** = background + 0.032 (capped at
  0.24). Both are asserted monotonic in lightness by a unit test.
- **Text** = the lightest colour (C ≤ 0.02), l starting in [0.86, 0.93], raised in fixed 0.01 steps
  (ceiling 0.97) until it clears **4.5:1 against the background, the surface *and* the glass
  composite** — the three pairs the §2.2 rules name, guaranteed together, not one at a time.
- **Muted** = text at alpha 0.62, raised in 0.02 steps until 4.5:1 on the background.
- **Accent** = max `weight × C` (ties broken on hex — deterministic), then the fit: measured against
  a neutral dark base (`#0b0b0d`) and a neutral light base (`#e8e6e3`); the stronger passes unless
  neither does, in which case lightness steps ±0.02 toward the stronger base until the pair passes.
  The demo palette's rust passes as-is (the probe reads accent `#c56a3e` in and out,
  accent-contrast `#0b0b0d`); the mid-tone fixture forces the stepping path in `theme.test.ts`.
- **Float sRGB compositing is the contract.** `compositeOver` keeps fractional channels (WebAIM
  arithmetic): rounding to bytes first turns the measured glass pair into 16.17:1. The five E2-S3
  pairs reproduce exactly — 15.79 / 15.01 / 16.20 / 6.40 / 8.46 — which `contrast.test.ts` and
  `theme.test.ts` both assert.

## 5. Browser probe (offline Chromium, built app)

Method (the E2-S5 technique, ADR-019): the built app served by a local Python server
(`/tmp/opencode/serve_probe2.py`), two probe pages assembled from `dist/index.html` (head script:
error capture, heartbeat, `history.replaceState` to the route, explicit `document.fonts.load`),
a 25-second image on a second local port so the document's `load` event fires after the probe writes
its JSON, and `chromium-browser --headless=new --dump-dom`. No request leaves localhost.

- **The `@supports` branch cannot be forced in headless Chromium** (no flag removes
  `backdrop-filter` support). Its shipping is evidenced by the dist CSS above; the active branch by
  the computed styles in §5. Stated rather than implied.
- The probe's `transferSize` is headers + body (~300 B over the on-disk size) — verified by
  comparing the probe log with `ls dist/assets/`.
- Probe pages and script lived in `/tmp/opencode/e2s6-probe/` (never committed); they are assembled
  from `dist/index.html` plus one 4.1 kB plain-JS file, reproducible from this report.
- Reference principles consulted for this story (DESIGN-REFERENCES §5/§7, sanitize-checked): WebAIM
  contrast thresholds (4.5:1 normal, 3:1 large/UI); Radix Colors' dark ramps and alpha variants —
  text guaranteed against its paired background, which is exactly the guarantee our loops build;
  NN/g's glassmorphism notes — blur handles busy photo backdrops, contrast must hold on the
  *composite*, reduced-transparency settings substitute a solid, and glass is used sparingly. They
  are cited in the pull request.
- CSS grew 9,177 → 13,482 B (+4.3 kB): the check page's utilities, the glass rules and the solid
  token. The entry chunk grew 283.41 → 283.58 kB (+170 B) with the fourth lazy route. Both are
  within the story's expectations; no dependencies were added.
